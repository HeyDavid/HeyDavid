Meet your barber

Fifteen years, two coasts, and a few hundred thousand cuts later — the philosophy hasn't changed: listen first, cut second. Every head is different, and the goal is a style you can actually do yourself on a Monday morning.

Trained in classic barbering and obsessed with the modern fade, the work lands somewhere between barbershop tradition and editorial precision. Come in with a reference photo or come in with nothing — either way, you leave looking like the best version of you.
